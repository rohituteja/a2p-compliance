Privacy Policy

Odie Agent (Stinkyserver) — operated by Rohit Tuteja (sole proprietor)

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What we collect

  • The mobile phone number of users who actively opt in to receive messages.
  • The content of messages sent to and received from opted-in users, retained as operational logs for troubleshooting and service improvement.

How we use it

  • To deliver the alert and notification messages you opted in to receive from the Odie Agent service.
  • To honor opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) requests.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or message content with any third party for marketing purposes.
  • Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Storage

Message logs are stored on the operator's private server (stinkyserver) and are accessible only to the operator. Logs are retained for a limited period for troubleshooting and then deleted.

Opting out

Text STOP to the sending number at any time to permanently opt out. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. Text HELP for assistance.
